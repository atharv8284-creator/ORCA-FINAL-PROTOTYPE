# Indradhanu Hackathon 2026: AI for Greener Tomorrow

## Project: ORCA
### Marine Ecosystem Reasoning with Collaborative Autonomous Agents

---

### The Problem: Marine Ecosystems in Crisis
Oceans cover 71% of Earth's surface and produce over 50% of our oxygen while absorbing 90% of excess planetary heat. Yet marine conservation remains reactive:
1. **Coral Bleaching**: Entire reef structures die before researchers receive delayed satellite anomaly reports.
2. **Expanding Dead Zones**: Benthic ocean areas experience sudden oxygen depletion (hypoxia) with fatal consequences for bottom-dwelling species.
3. **Illegal, Unreported & Unregulated (IUU) Fishing**: "Dark vessels" operate inside Marine Protected Areas by disabling tracking beacons.
4. **Data Silos**: Sensor buoys, satellite altimetry, and submersibles collect isolated data without unified collaborative intelligence.

---

### The ORCA Solution
**ORCA** introduces **Collaborative Autonomous Agents** for marine stewardship. Instead of isolated dashboards, ORCA deploys specialized AI agents that continuously perceive, exchange hypotheses, and collectively deliberate on ecological interventions:

- 🪸 **Reef Sentinel**: Detects thermal stress and chlorophyll loss in corals before irreversible bleaching occurs.
- 🌡️ **Thermal Recon**: Tracks thermocline inversions and computes Degree Heating Weeks (DHW).
- 🔊 **Acoustic Profiler**: Triangulates propulsion signatures of unflagged vessels inside marine sanctuaries.
- 🌿 **Blue Carbon Observer**: Quantifies blue carbon sequestration across seagrass meadows and mangrove biomes.
- 🫧 **Patrol Swarm**: Sniffs out oxygen-depleted dead zones to activate autonomous mitigation procedures.

---

### Impact on "AI for Greener Tomorrow"
- **Early Interventions**: Automated alerts and robotic shade / diffuser triggers allow mitigation before ecological collapse.
- **Explainable Multi-Agent AI**: Transparent deliberation chains ensure marine authorities understand the scientific hypothesis behind every recommended action.
- **Scalable Geospatial Intelligence**: Backed by PostGIS, ORCA scales seamlessly from localized marine sanctuaries to global oceanic basins.
